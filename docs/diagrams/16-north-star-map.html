The rug label, and the seven concerns under it

The goal has not changed since it was set: make the staircase / up-only ramp actually get labelled rug. Everything built since is either raw material for that verdict, the plumbing that makes the material trustworthy, or the lane that would consume the signal. This page is the one context that ties them together — the dependency spine, a readiness×evidence matrix for evaluating what to do next, and the register with each concern's state, blocker and artifact.

The spine — what unblocks what

Read it left to right: the verdict is not blocked by more code — it is blocked by the absence of a hundred minutes of volume-bearing history per token. The registry (shipped) and the standardizer (P2) make the data trustworthy; the ramp collection is what makes the verdict possible at all.

Where each concern sits — readiness against evidence

The matrix is the evaluation: one thing we want is unmeasured and blocked (the verdict), four things are measured and ready, and the empty top-left cell means nothing we are about to act on rests on an assumption we never checked.
