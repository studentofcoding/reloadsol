Where the rug label stands, and what's next

The goal has not moved: make the staircase / up-only ramp actually get labelled rug. What exists now is the material for that verdict, the plumbing that makes the material trustworthy, and — deliberately — the measurement that decides whether the verdict may act. Nothing enforces yet, and that is a decision, not a gap.

The path as built

Everything left of the gate is done and running. The gate is the whole point of the current phase: neither the trip write nor the ML lane moves until the measurement clears its floor.

What's next

  1. Let the soak produce the first verdict. Rows become labellable 30 minutes after they are written, so the floor (30 labelled / 5 collapses) is reachable within a day. The harness then states a lift or no lift — and because the acceptance rule is that days must agree, the honest answer is a multi-day one. No code, just time.
  2. Explain series_fed: 21/150. Either those tokens are simply not rising — the band correctly scores 0 without a price rise — or the series is too sparse for the band to see anything. The two causes need opposite responses, and the answer decides whether the band is measurable on this population at all. Diagnostic: bars per mint, 5m buckets, and mcap gain across the 129 that scored 0. Worth doing before reading much into the soak.
  3. Enforce, then feed the ML shadow lane. GATED Only if the harness clears its floor with a lift over base rate. RUG_SIGNAL_MODE=enforce is the deliberate keystroke; the kill switch stays. The lane that would consume the verdict currently runs 100% source='identity'.
