The staircase that nothing called a rug

Two copycat tokens (MINDX, SpaceX) printed a flat thin-volume base then a vertical staircase up. Every surface read inconclusive. None of that copy was ours: it was the RugCheck + dev-reputation chip — 39/100 is RugCheck's own score_normalised, the risk words are RugCheck's own, and inconclusive is the dev scorer's sample < 5 gate. Our candle spine says nothing about a ramp: dump_10m needs a drop, wick_reject needs upper wicks, volume_death inverts, and up_only_10 demands ten of ten green bars — exactly what a staircase with pauses is not.

The scorer and its write path

The write is immediate and lands in the shared rug registry — so the tracker label, the signal list, the rug list and the OHLC corpus all move together, and nothing can call a ramp a rug on one surface while another still says inconclusive.

Score anatomy — a live ramp reaches 90 against an 80 threshold

Volume and liquidity are bands, not gates: 0 at the safest reading and the full weight at the riskiest, so a merely flat-ish ramp lands part-way instead of all-or-nothing. The liquidity band is linear from a 10% liq/mcap anchor — 5% scores 10, 1.5% scores 17 — and the volume band averages two sub-signals: how far volume expansion lags price, and how dead-flat volume is.

Calibration — what the data said, and refused to say

BLOCKER

Our 1m series has no volume

  • token_ohlc_bars.volume is NULL on 875,535 of 875,535 rows.
  • The 15s sampler writes a Jupiter spot price into open/high/low/close and never writes volume.
  • So on the own-1m fallback the 30-point volume band is inert — a ramp tops out at 60 and can never trip. Fix the sampler to unblock calibration.

REFUTED

Thin volume ≠ risky

  • volume_at_entry / entry_mcap, 4,407 outcomes, p50 by bucket: crash 0.70 · loss 0.34 · flat 0.24 · win<100% 0.44 · win≥100% 0.75.
  • Thin volume tracks flat, not risky — both tails are the most active.
  • So the band is built on volume shape vs price, not on "low volume = danger".

INCONCLUSIVE

No separation on the corpus

  • signal_ohlc_labels, 1,712 rug vs 3,359 rising cards: expansionRisk p50 0.88 vs 0.96 (means 0.54 vs 0.62) — directionally inverted; volCv p50 0.90 vs 0.86.
  • The corpus anchors rug cards at the dump, so it cannot show a live pre-dump ramp.
  • Verdict: the anchors are chosen, not fitted. Fit them once volume lands.
