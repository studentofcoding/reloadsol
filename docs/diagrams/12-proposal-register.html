What each one adds on top of flat

Four entries move the needle, and the chart prices them against flat rather than against today — because flat itself is the change worth having (+1.531 over today) and every other proposal is a tweak layered on it. Three add; one subtracts. Note the combination is less than the sum of its parts: P3 + P4 is a naive 0.391 against a measured 0.285, because folding and the stop both remove the same losing trades.

What this means

  • Only three proposals compose, and only two of them are measured. P1 (flat), P3 (fold), P4 (stop), plus P6 which shipped. Everything else is either subsumed (P2) or a decision that rides along with another (P7). Shipping all ten is not the plan; shipping three is.
  • The combination is worth less than the sum of its parts, and that is not a rounding error. Measured +0.285 over flat against a naive 0.391 — folding and the stop both remove the same losing trades, so the overlap is real. Any estimate that adds proposal gains together overstates the result by roughly a quarter.
  • Two of the ten are explicitly negative-proposals, and that is the majority of the intellectual work here. P10 rejects a knob that looked plausible and measurably subtracts; P2's condition C-2 shows the guard becomes a no-op the moment the fold ships. Deciding not to build is the deliverable, not the consolation.
  • P7 is not a proposal, it is a bill. Deleting the multiplier raises the average stake ~2.6× on its own, so the base-stake question arrives automatically. Worth stating plainly: at a risk-parity base of 0.0019 the combination still returns 1.002 SOL against today's 0.822, at 89.4% ROI against 64.7% — so the cut is a scaling decision, not a reason to abandon P1. But peak exposure is a concurrency constraint that ROI does not capture, and that check needs the open-position series.
  • The one number on this page that is not a result is +0.247. It is a bound obtained by clamping realised losses, which assumes no trade that fell past −16 would have recovered. A real sweep has to replay candles. Everything else priced here is measured on the actual closes.
  • And the whole register rests on a two-day sample that grew while it was being written. The window is open, so the close count moved 646 → 651 → 667 across the three passes that produced these pages. Every figure here is one consistent snapshot of 667 closes; the direction across ten proposals is stable, the third significant figure is not. Enough to act on the measured three, not enough to size a bankroll on.
