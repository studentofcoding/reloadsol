Findings

  • The mean is not a result. Every headline average in this table is dominated by a handful of outsized winners. Ranking by mean would put S200 (+127%) above S150 (+134% mean, +8.8% median) — ranking by median, S150 leads.
  • mcap/search is the only family with a positive median across variants — and it is the one that was parked from the assessment until exits were valued and features.exec landed. Both now verified.
  • GMGN KOL is running and losing. 13–15% win, medians −51% and −60%, no exec coverage. Nothing here is a small-sample accident: 85 trades combined, all on the wrong side.
  • 31 of 85 closes in mcap_enter_at_80 are exactly 0% — over a third. Zero-return closes inflate the trade count while contributing nothing, and they dilute the win rate. Worth tracing to the close path before that strategy is trusted.
  • att_rh is break-even at the median, not broken. I flagged its 0.0 median as a data smell; the per-trade distribution says otherwise — only 9 of 3,480 closes are exactly zero, so the median is genuinely ≈0 (a −0.04 rounds to 0.0). It is a high-frequency, break-even desk whose total comes entirely from the right tail.
  • Two strategies are silently stalled — social_only_fomo_gt7 (last close 09-28) and scalper (09-24) — and nothing surfaces that. A worker that stops is indistinguishable from a strategy that finds nothing.
  • exec coverage is absent for att_rh, both GMGN families, social and scalper (0 rows each), so those families have no modelled-drag evidence at all — we cannot say what the realistic slippage would have been.
  • Regime context is a close-time stamp, not a column. market_regime_tags is one row per day and the outcome writer resolves it at close, so no column is needed — but the daily row is written by the mcap sim worker, so a day it does not run has no regime at all. History is not backfillable.
